I am Hojeong Lee, a Ph.D. candidate at HCI Tech Lab, Robotics Program, KAIST.

I aim to enhance the use of human hands by designing auxiliary hardware and a control system.
Therefore, my research focuses on wearable haptic gloves, soft actuators, and wearable ergonomics.
I am currently working on polymer-based soft actuators to develop compact and effective wearable gloves.
