Hojeong Lee

I am a researcher, designer, and engineer.

My research focuses on wearable haptic gloves, soft actuators, and HRI.
I aim to enhance the use of human hands by designing auxiliary hardware and a control system for natural haptic interaction.

Graduated from Seoul National University with a B.F.A. in Industrial Design, I am currently a Ph.D. candidate at HCI Tech Lab, Robotics Program, KAIST.

My works have been published in IEEE WHC, IMWUT, IEEE ISMAR, demo at CHI, and exhibited at DDP Seoul.
